Welcome

A place built around slowness

Aurelia Bay was built on the idea that a resort doesn't need to fill every hour to be worth the trip. Rooms open onto private balconies facing the water, the tide pool stays warm past sunset, and the kitchen cooks whatever the local boats brought in that morning.

We keep the property small on purpose — forty keys, never more — so the staff know your name by the second morning and the beach never feels like it belongs to anyone but you.
